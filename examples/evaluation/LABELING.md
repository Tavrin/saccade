# Optional human spot checks

No human labeling is required for the constructed-truth evaluation under design section 16. Construction records and preserved outcomes supply truth. Unknown recorded truth is excluded, without guessing from family membership or model output.

The earlier `pilot.py` workbench remains available for optional human checks of the recorded corpus. Its structured-first assessment precedes pixels, records model/mapping/implementation exposure, validates the five-question catalog and keeps labels private. It has no provider dispatch path.

If human labels are later added, the original single-labeler and delayed test–retest rules apply to those labels. The workbench retains both stages and requires seven days between the initial held-out subset and its retest. They are separate from constructed truth and never overwrite it.

Provider egress for every case is already authorized by section 16. Public artifacts continue to contain only aggregates and hashes.
