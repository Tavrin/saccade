# R12 decisions

The owner’s design section 16 supplies egress authorization and removes mandatory human labeling. Every case is eligible for private Jev and Gemini evaluation. Publication retains hashes and aggregates only. The earlier workbench is optional and dispatch-disabled.

Whole scenes remain in one split. Scene aliases for recorded Bistro cases are grouped together. Shared image hashes across splits are rejected. This is stricter than grouping only by scene and change family, because several constructed interventions use the same real source capture. Frames, crops, repeats and presentation orders do not increase independent support.

The available newer renderer binary referenced a deleted source checkout. Captures therefore use the intact pinned renderer snapshot through the required current wrapper and GPU lease. Its binary hash and successful wrapper receipts remain bound to the corpus. This evaluation does not claim to capture the current renderer revision.

Documented renderer controls supply repeated captures and no-effect debug-mode toggles. A single leased variant capture supplies native exposure and volumetric-fog changes in the post-processing scene, each against a matched Fidelity control. The other intended interventions specify display-pixel multiplication, a channel grade and a uniform composite veil; those exact operations use deterministic image-space transforms. Image-space changes also supply lossless PNG storage optimizations, regional texture/tonemap defects, HUD text/icon changes, float EXRs and multi-frame sequences. EXRs reconstruct display-linear samples from real captures and preserve float samples; they do not recover scene radiance. Temporal cases are constructed sequences of independently rendered static captures, without continuous-simulation claims.

The performance cases retain actual captured frame measurements in typed performance v2 sidecars. Constructed missing or failed clock checks are interpreted by the production performance reader. They establish incomparability, without inventing timing or speedups. Metadata confounds change an exposure setting without declaring it. Recorded answers require preserved identity, ablation or deterministic qualification evidence; all other answers remain unknown and are excluded from accuracy.

One HTTP request batches the applicable questions for one case. The frozen plan counts the actual adapters and leaves bounded retry reserves. Every real request, retry and fallback probe uses the R11 evaluation ledger, with immutable caps of 400 Jev and 250 Gemini attempts. Model revisions, observation content hashes, transforms and fallback outcomes remain attributed. Direct Jev, enriched Jev, enriched Jev without project priors, Gemini alone and deterministic rules have separate scores.

Both orders use the same actual Gemini model and revision or remain unresolved. An abstention in one order counts as order sensitivity. Missing responses are unavailable or deferred, rather than fabricated abstentions. Calibration uses only its designated split. No question qualifies without all section 9 gates, including 50 held-out applicable cases and a declared important-miss tolerance. This corpus cannot establish those gates; conservative review remains the policy.

No git mutation, image publication, question change after freeze or P5 autopilot is part of this lane.

## R12 expansion decisions

The paid-tier results and original 86-case freeze remain the baseline. The expansion writes a second manifest and a `freeze-amendment.json` seal; it never rewrites the original case list, job plan, rubric, or paid results. New provider jobs are only planned. This lane makes no provider calls.

The requested Moss checkout's capture wrapper predates the timer-quantum and performance-sidecar producer. For new native performance captures, the expansion uses the newer `main-fix` Moss wrapper and its integration renderer binary through the same GPU lease. The private run configuration records both paths and hashes; the public amendment records hashes only. Reversal cost: cheap while unmerged. Using the old wrapper would produce no usable `saccade-perf.v2` evidence.

The integration binary is a shared path and can be replaced by another lane. Once the first two expansion batches proved the same executable SHA-256 in their capture metadata, a byte-identical, read-only copy was pinned in the private derived-data root for later batches. The binary embeds a dirty source build identity from a different Moss lane; this is retained as an identity and replay limitation. The expansion does not claim current-main renderer acceptance. Reversal cost: cheap while unmerged.

Each visual addition is a separately declared image-space operation against a real Moss frame, with a unique intervention and sealed packet. The old held-out terrain scene remains held out, both old calibration scenes remain calibration, and the separate Sponza project supplies development cases when captured. New native performance cases use distinct camera and renderer-control pairs, two unchanged captures to measure repeat noise, and a third unchanged no-effect arm. The measured comparison supplies the performance disposition; a rejected or unknown qualification is labelled `collect_more_evidence`. A speedup is never inferred from an image or invented timing. Reversal cost: cheap while unmerged.

The frozen additions select 90 held-out, 36 calibration, and 54 development cases, matching the design's 100:40:60 split ratio for new cases. Some private calibration and development capture variants and prepared cases are excluded from the amendment. Their views do not increase support. Reversal cost: cheap while unmerged.

The first two native capture trials recorded a GPU timestamp quantum but did not qualify: sampled-frame clock windows were incomplete and probe warmup remained unconverged. Those results are retained as private evidence. The expansion never changes qualification fields to turn them into accepted measurements. A completed 1080p Sponza trial with explicit GPU warmup passed every producer qualification check; the 4K trial timed out and is excluded. The qualified cohort uses that completed setup, and the amendment reports pair comparability separately from producer qualification. Reversal cost: free.

The first full Sponza batch encountered `probe_unconverged` on its `sky=off` arm after 998 warmup frames. The failed batch is excluded. The development plan omits that switch and adds deterministic visual interventions to retain the selected split ratio. Reversal cost: cheap while unmerged.

A later five-camera batch stopped at camera four with `receiver_unconverged`. The completed two-camera, twelve-variant batch has real timer and GPU-clock files, but its per-variant producer qualifications are rejected or unknown: sampled-frame qualification fields are missing and completion intervals do not bind the windows. Those eight selected performance cases remain `collect_more_evidence`. The prior single-capture Sponza trial qualified; independent single repeat and bloom-off captures are required before claiming a qualified comparison. Reversal cost: cheap while unmerged.

The independently rendered repeat, bloom-off and two unchanged controls have qualified producer sidecars. The wrapper's first two unchanged controls were cached copies; they were discarded and recaptured with `MOSS_CAPTURE_FORCE=1`, yielding distinct capture hashes. The cold-cache trial, repeat and bloom-off do not qualify as pairs under the production reader: dynamic pass-gap sets differ, and the trial's configuration differs from warmed captures. The selected qualified pair uses three warmed, unchanged single captures. Its declared projection keeps the original measured frame values, raw samples, timer quantum, hardware, qualification and capture hashes; it omits unstable pass/gap terms and computes a stable configuration hash from `cost_card.config` and semantic scene/camera/environment/override inputs, excluding the dynamic measured `render_passes` field. The original unprojected sidecars and capture manifests are privately retained and hash-bound. Production noise and compare both mark this projected whole-frame pair qualified and within measured noise, but its unattributed frame bound blocks a no-effect conclusion; `perf.interpret.v1` remains `collect_more_evidence`. No qualified pass-level or causal claim follows. Reversal cost: cheap while unmerged.

Older recorded private packets are missing from the current workspace. The expansion validator checks their exact public manifest prefix and original seal, and rehashes every new generated file and source. It reports that old source-level replay limit rather than claiming the old private packets were revalidated. Reversal cost: cheap while unmerged.
