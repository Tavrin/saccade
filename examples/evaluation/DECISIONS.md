# R12 decisions

The owner’s design section 16 supplies egress authorization and removes mandatory human labeling. Every case is eligible for private Jev and Gemini evaluation. Publication retains hashes and aggregates only. The earlier workbench is optional and dispatch-disabled.

Whole scenes remain in one split. Scene aliases for recorded Bistro cases are grouped together. Shared image hashes across splits are rejected. This is stricter than grouping only by scene and change family, because several constructed interventions use the same real source capture. Frames, crops, repeats and presentation orders do not increase independent support.

The available newer renderer binary referenced a deleted source checkout. Captures therefore use the intact pinned renderer snapshot through the required current wrapper and GPU lease. Its binary hash and successful wrapper receipts remain bound to the corpus. This evaluation does not claim to capture the current renderer revision.

Documented renderer controls supply repeated captures and no-effect debug-mode toggles. A single leased variant capture supplies native exposure and volumetric-fog changes in the post-processing scene, each against a matched Fidelity control. The other intended interventions specify display-pixel multiplication, a channel grade and a uniform composite veil; those exact operations use deterministic image-space transforms. Image-space changes also supply lossless PNG storage optimizations, regional texture/tonemap defects, HUD text/icon changes, float EXRs and multi-frame sequences. EXRs reconstruct display-linear samples from real captures and preserve float samples; they do not recover scene radiance. Temporal cases are constructed sequences of independently rendered static captures, without continuous-simulation claims.

The performance cases retain actual captured frame measurements in typed performance v2 sidecars. Constructed missing or failed clock checks are interpreted by the production performance reader. They establish incomparability, without inventing timing or speedups. Metadata confounds change an exposure setting without declaring it. Recorded answers require preserved identity, ablation or deterministic qualification evidence; all other answers remain unknown and are excluded from accuracy.

One HTTP request batches the applicable questions for one case. The frozen plan counts the actual adapters and leaves bounded retry reserves. Every real request, retry and fallback probe uses the R11 evaluation ledger, with immutable caps of 400 Jev and 250 Gemini attempts. Model revisions, observation content hashes, transforms and fallback outcomes remain attributed. Direct Jev, enriched Jev, enriched Jev without project priors, Gemini alone and deterministic rules have separate scores.

Both orders use the same actual Gemini model and revision or remain unresolved. An abstention in one order counts as order sensitivity. Missing responses are unavailable or deferred, rather than fabricated abstentions. Calibration uses only its designated split. No question qualifies without all section 9 gates, including 50 held-out applicable cases and a declared important-miss tolerance. This corpus cannot establish those gates; conservative review remains the policy.

No git mutation, image publication, question change after freeze or P5 autopilot is part of this lane.
