# Paired performance evidence

Existing comparisons and ablation reports can include a robust frame-timing
estimate and uncertainty interval. Supply `saccade-perf-pairs.json` alongside
the candidate's usual `saccade-perf.json`. No benchmark is executed by Saccade.
The sidecar's two aggregate-file hashes must match the exact performance files
being compared; stale, malformed or incomplete series reject performance
qualification rather than silently reverting to an aggregate speedup.

Before acquisition, declare a `saccade-perf-plan.v1` with `independent_unit` set
to `run`, `stopping_rule` set to `fixed_count`, and every pair ID and `ab`/`ba`
order in the `pairs` array. Use 6–128 independently acquired matched run pairs,
include both acquisition orders, and predeclare `confidence` (0.8–0.99),
`resamples` (1024–8192) and `seed`. Freeze its canonical SHA-256 before collecting
measurements. Saccade checks the complete count, IDs, order and plan hash. It
cannot independently prove when an external producer froze its plan or whether
runs are statistically independent. Correlated frames cannot substitute for
independent runs. Do not rerun ordinary intervals until a desired result appears.

The `saccade-perf-pairs.v1` sidecar contains `before_perf_sha256`,
`after_perf_sha256` (`sha256:` hashes of exact bytes), the frozen `plan`, its
`plan_sha256`, and `observations` with `pair_id`, `order`, `before_ms` and
`after_ms`. Times must be finite and strictly positive. Arrays preserve the
plan's acquisition order. [Schemas](contracts.md) describe the exact shapes.

For each pair, the estimator takes candidate minus reference. It is the median
of all Walsh averages of those paired differences, including diagonal pairs:
`(d_i + d_j)/2` for `i <= j`. This paired Hodges–Lehmann estimate is a robust
pseudomedian shift; under skew it is not necessarily the population median.
A separate relative estimate applies the same estimator to paired log-ratios
and reports `100 * (exp(effect) - 1)`.

Bootstrap draws use the prespecified SplitMix64 stream with rejection-sampled
indices. Each draw resamples entire pairs with replacement, using the same
indices for difference and log-ratio estimates. The recorded interval is a
percentile interval with linearly interpolated empirical quantiles. Seed, draw
count, confidence, plan/sample identities and raw observations are retained in
the effect packet. Equal-valued bootstrap distributions are explicitly degenerate and
cannot support a new frame performance verdict. There is no BCa, block bootstrap
or sequential-confidence claim.

When the sidecar exists, capture and repeat-noise qualification still apply.
A faster/slower verdict requires the entire nondegenerate frame interval to lie
beyond the declared noise/materiality threshold. An interval inside the noise
band is within measured noise; a crossing interval is inconclusive. Raw
aggregate values stay in the report, and term/counter deltas remain aggregate
diagnostics. Historical inputs without a sidecar keep their previous aggregate
and noise rules and have no robust uncertainty estimate. They do not acquire
new confidence evidence by being read by a newer binary.

Constructed validation covers constants, ties, outliers, paired log-ratios,
independent NumPy reference values (regenerated by
`scripts/reference-wave3-stats.py`), complete-plan rejection, deterministic
bootstrap and symmetric null/shift simulations. Those simulations provide a
limited sanity check, not universal interval coverage or renderer timing
qualification. Robust statistics do not repair workload mismatch, thermal drift,
clock variation or run-order bias. No timing benchmarks were run in this lane.

References: [NIST Hodges–Lehmann definition](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/hodglehm.htm)
and [SciPy paired bootstrap](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.bootstrap.html).
